About TKR Al Khobar

TKR — Tahir Khan Restaurant — brings its Pakistani and Afghan cooking to Al Khobar, on Prince Majid Street in Al Khobar Al Shamalia. The branch had its grand opening on Thursday 12 November 2026.

The menu covers signature dum pukht and platters, Shinwari and Lahori karahi, BBQ, Chapli Kabab, rice, tandoor breads, seafood, soups, salads, Chinese and Continental dishes, desserts and hot and cold drinks.

The restaurant is part of the TKR family founded by Tahir Khan, with branches in Pakistan, the UK and Saudi Arabia. The Al Khobar listing notes a wheelchair-accessible entrance and parking.
